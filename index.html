📖 Frequently Asked Questions

    
      
How does Protein Cycling work?
      

The app guides you through phases of higher and lower protein intake based on your activity and goals. This helps you build muscle when needed and supports cellular repair during rest phases — solving the protein paradox.

    
    
      
Do I need to track everything manually?
      

Nope! Just log your meals like you normally would. The app analyzes your protein quality, leucine content, and timing automatically to give you real-time feedback.

    
    
      
Is this based on real science?
      

Yes. Our recommendations are derived from peer-reviewed longevity and nutrition research, including studies on mTOR pathways, leucine thresholds, and protein timing.

    
    
      
Can I use this while vegetarian / vegan?
      

Absolutely. The app adjusts recommendations based on your dietary preferences and helps you optimize plant-based protein combinations to hit your leucine targets.
